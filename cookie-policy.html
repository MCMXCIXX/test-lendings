Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember your preferences between visits, and provide site owners with information about how the site is used. We also use similar technologies, such as pixels and local storage, which work in a comparable way to cookies.

2. How We Use Cookies

ClearRay Solar Advisors uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

CategoryPurposeExamplesCan Be Disabled?
Necessary / Essential Required for core Site functionality, such as multi-step form progress, security, and remembering your cookie preferences Session identifier, cookie-consent preference, security/anti-fraud tokens No
Analytics / Performance Help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve content and usability Google Analytics-type measurement cookies, page-load and scroll-depth tracking Yes
Marketing / Advertising Used to measure ad campaign effectiveness and, where enabled, show relevant ads on other sites Ad-platform retargeting pixels, conversion-tracking tags Yes
Preferences / Functional Remember choices you've made, such as your cookie consent settings Consent preference cookie, form-progress local storage Yes (except the consent record itself)

3. How to Control Cookies

When you first visit our Site, you will see a cookie consent banner allowing you to Accept All, Reject Non-Essential, or customize your preferences by category. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the site footer, which reopens the preference panel described above.

You can also control cookies directly through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. Note that disabling necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Site — including the multi-step eligibility form — from functioning correctly.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Site are placed by third-party analytics and advertising services we use, such as web analytics platforms and ad-network conversion pixels. These third parties operate under their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review on their respective websites. We do not control the cookies these third parties set once you have consented to their use.

5. How Long Cookies Last

Session cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period — typically up to 12 months for analytics and marketing cookies, or until you clear them manually — after which they expire or are refreshed on your next visit, subject to your saved preferences.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the technologies we use or applicable legal requirements. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above, and material changes may also be communicated via an updated consent prompt.

7. Contact Us

Questions about our use of cookies? Contact us at privacy@clearraysolar.com.
