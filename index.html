Why homeowners are looking into this now

Utility rates keep climbing — and most bills don't explain why

Between grid infrastructure costs, fuel price swings, and rising demand, residential electricity rates have increased in the majority of U.S. states over the past several years. For many households, the monthly bill has become one of the least predictable line items in the budget.

Rates rarely go down

Utility rate increases are typically approved incrementally and rarely reversed, which means a bill that feels manageable today can climb steadily over a 10–20 year horizon.

Incentives are easy to miss

Federal tax credits, state rebates, and utility-specific programs change from year to year and vary by ZIP code — most homeowners never find out which ones actually apply to their address.

Quotes are hard to compare

Reaching out to installers one by one is slow and makes it difficult to compare financing structures, equipment, and warranties side by side.

What homeowners typically ask about

What a home solar system can offer

Every property is different, and a licensed installer will give you exact numbers for your roof, usage, and local rates. In general, homeowners who move forward tend to be interested in the following.

Lower, more predictable bills

Generating a portion of your own electricity can reduce reliance on utility rate increases. Actual savings depend on system size, usage, financing terms, and local rates, and are not guaranteed.

$0-down financing options

Many installers in our network offer solar loans or leases that require no upfront payment. Approval and terms depend on credit and program availability in your state.

Available incentives

The federal solar Investment Tax Credit and select state or utility rebates may reduce the net cost of a system for qualifying homeowners. Eligibility rules change and should be confirmed with a tax professional.

Backup power readiness

Many systems can be paired with battery storage, which may provide backup power during outages depending on the equipment and configuration chosen.

Warrantied equipment

Reputable installers typically offer 10–25 year warranties on panels, inverters, and workmanship. Warranty terms vary by manufacturer and installer.

No-obligation quotes

Reviewing a quote does not commit you to installation. You can compare options and walk away at any point in the process.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost anything to check my eligibility?

No. Completing the form on this page is free and does not obligate you to purchase anything. There is no fee to be matched with a partner or to receive a quote.

Will someone call me, and how soon?

If your ZIP code and answers indicate you may be eligible, a specialist from our partner network typically reaches out within 1 business day by phone or email, using the contact details you provide on the final step.

Do I need to buy anything today?

No. This form is a screening step to see what may be available for your home. Any decision to move forward with a quote, financing, or installation happens later, directly between you and the licensed installer, on your own timeline.

Is ClearRay Solar Advisors a government program?

No. ClearRay Solar Advisors is a private lead-generation service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government agency. Some homeowners may separately qualify for government or utility incentive programs, which a licensed installer can explain in detail.

What happens to the information I submit?

We use it to determine whether solar options may be available in your area and, if so, to connect you with relevant partners who may contact you about your inquiry. Full details are available in our Privacy Policy.

How much can I actually expect to save?

Savings depend on your current utility rates, roof orientation, shading, system size, and financing terms, so we can't quote a number without a site-specific assessment. Any savings estimate you receive from a partner should be treated as an estimate, not a guarantee.

Will requesting a quote affect my credit?

Requesting information through this form does not involve a credit check. If you choose to apply for solar financing later with a partner, that lender will explain whether and how their application process affects your credit.

Can I stop the process at any time?

Yes. You are never obligated to continue past any step, and you can ask any partner who contacts you to stop further communication. See our Privacy Policy for details on managing your information.
