1. Introduction

ClearRay Solar Advisors ("ClearRay," "we," "us," or "our") operates clearraysolar.com (the "Site"), an online service that helps U.S. homeowners find out whether solar energy programs, financing, and installers may be available for their address. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit the Site or submit a request through our eligibility form, how we use and share that information, and the choices and rights available to you.

By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the Site or submit information through our form.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you complete our multi-step eligibility form, we collect the information you choose to enter, which may include:

  • Whether you own or rent your home
  • Your approximate average monthly electric bill
  • Your ZIP code or property address
  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number

If you contact us directly by email (for example, to ask a question or exercise a privacy right), we also collect the information you include in that communication.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, time spent on the Site, and links clicked
  • Referring website, search terms, or advertising campaign that led you to the Site
  • Cookie identifiers and similar tracking data (see Section 7 and our Cookie Policy)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Determine whether solar programs, incentives, or financing options may be available at your address
  • Match you with pre-screened solar installers and financing partners in our network who service your area
  • Communicate with you about your submission, including by phone, email, or text message
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site's functionality and content
  • Analyze aggregate traffic patterns and measure the performance of our advertising campaigns
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or abusive activity on the Site
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal requests

4. How We Share Your Information

ClearRay does not sell your personal information to unrelated third parties for their own independent marketing purposes. We do share information in the following circumstances:

Solar Partners and Service Providers

When you submit a request through our Site, we share the information you provide — such as your name, contact details, ZIP code, and answers to qualifying questions — with our network of pre-screened solar installers and financing partners who may be able to service your address. These partners may contact you directly by phone, email, or text message to discuss your inquiry, provide a quote, or explain financing options. We do not control how a partner uses information after it is shared with them for purposes connected to your request; each partner's own privacy practices apply to their direct interactions with you.

Analytics and Advertising Providers

We work with third-party analytics and advertising providers who help us understand how visitors use the Site and measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. These providers may receive information such as device identifiers, IP address, and browsing behavior on the Site, subject to your cookie preferences (see our Cookie Policy).

Legal and Safety Purposes

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud.

Business Transfers

If ClearRay is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information you have provided may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality arrangements.

5. Data Retention

We retain the personal information you submit for as long as needed to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected — typically up to 24 months from your last interaction with us — after which it is securely deleted or anonymized, unless a longer retention period is required to comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations, or to resolve disputes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities, including for targeted advertising
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, where applicable under state law (note: we do not sell personal information as defined under most state privacy laws)
  • Lodge a complaint with your state attorney general or applicable data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@clearraysolar.com. We will respond within 30 days or the timeframe required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember your preferences, and measure advertising performance. Categories, purposes, and your choices are described in full in our Cookie Policy. You can manage your preferences at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the site footer.

8. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit via HTTPS, access controls limiting who within our organization and partner network can view submitted information, and regular review of our data handling practices. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page; your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
